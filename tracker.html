Fit Scoring Guide — how to score a role
  • +2 if the role involves strategy, advisory, market intelligence, research, or business analysis.
  • +2 if it involves AI, fintech, cyber, VC, digital infrastructure, technology, or a Southeast Asia focus.
  • +2 if it values research, insights, data, risk, investigation, or structured analysis.
  • +1 if salary likely meets your target.
  • +1 if the company brand helps long-term positioning.
  • -2 if the role is mostly Scrum, backlog, customer support, project coordination, or pure delivery work.
8–10 — strong fit. Apply seriously.
6–7 — possible fit. Apply if bandwidth allows.
1–5 — low priority. Deprioritize or skip.
